The problem

Your organisation knows more than any single person in it. The trouble is finding it.

Work tracked in one tool, conversations in another, evidence in a third, decisions in someone's head. By the time you've found the answer, the moment has passed.

— 01

Knowledge fragmented

Work in one tool, conversations in another, evidence in a third. Nothing speaks to anything else, and the synthesis happens in your team's heads.

— 02

Reports take days

Board updates, donor reports, client reviews, leadership summaries. Every report becomes a multi-day excavation across spreadsheets, drives, and email threads.

— 03

Accountability invisible

Who promised what to whom. What evidence exists. Did the follow-up happen. When the answer lives in someone's inbox, accountability is unprovable.

The MoonDesk way

One brain. Three powers.

MoonDesk pulls your scattered knowledge into a single intelligence layer that does three things exceptionally well, so you can stop coordinating tools and start running your organisation.

  • Draft email replies grounded in real context
  • Create tasks and assign owners from a single prompt
  • Schedule meetings with all the right context attached
  • Multi-step playbooks across email, calendar, tasks, and CRM

03 Remember everything

Every commitment, every decision, every piece of evidence.

MoonDesk doesn't just track work. It tracks the proof. Evidence linked to outcomes. Decisions linked to discussions. Follow-ups tied to commitments. When your board, funder, or client asks, you have receipts.

  • Task completion requires evidence. Files, links, approval.
  • Every action logged with timestamp, actor, and context
  • Manager approval flows for sensitive deliverables
  • Export-ready audit trail for any stakeholder
