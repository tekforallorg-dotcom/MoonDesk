The problem

Your organisation knows more than any single person in it. The trouble is finding it.

Programmes get tracked in one tool, conversations in another, evidence in a third, decisions in someone's head. By the time you've found the answer, the moment has passed.

— 01

Knowledge fragmented

Programmes in one tool, conversations in another, evidence in a third. Nothing speaks to anything else, and the synthesis happens in your team's heads.

— 02

Reports take days

Donor updates, board summaries, programme reviews — every report becomes a multi-day excavation across spreadsheets, drives, and email threads.

— 03

Accountability invisible

Who promised what to whom? What evidence exists? Did the follow-up happen? When the answer lives in someone's inbox, accountability is unprovable.

The MoonDesk way

One brain. Three powers.

MoonDesk pulls your scattered knowledge into a single intelligence layer that does three things exceptionally well — so you can stop coordinating tools and start running your organisation.

01 Answers

Ask anything. Get a cited answer in seconds.

MoonDesk reads everything your team produces — programmes, tasks, conversations, documents, stakeholder records — and answers your questions with citations to the exact source. No more "let me check and get back to you."

  • Hybrid semantic + structured retrieval across all your data
  • Every fact cited back to its source — task, email, document
  • Voice and text input, on desktop and mobile
  • Works in seconds, not minutes

02 Actions

Don't just know. Act.

Once MoonDesk surfaces what needs doing, you can do it from the same surface — draft an email, create a task, schedule a meeting, send a follow-up. Every action requires your approval. Nothing fires autonomously.

  • Draft email replies grounded in real context
  • Create tasks and assign owners from a single prompt
  • Schedule meetings with all the right context attached
  • Approval-first — you stay in the loop

03 Accountability

Every commitment, every decision, every piece of evidence.

MoonDesk doesn't just track tasks — it tracks the proof. Evidence linked to outcomes, decisions linked to discussions, follow-ups tied to commitments. When your funder asks, you have receipts.

  • Task completion requires evidence — files, links, approval
  • Every action logged with timestamp, actor, and context
  • Manager approval workflows for sensitive deliverables
  • Export-ready audit trail for funders and boards

Security

Trust isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Enterprise-grade security defaults from day one. We don't sell your data. We don't train on it. We don't share it with anyone you didn't authorise.

Row-level security

Every query scoped to your org at the database layer. Cross-tenant access is impossible.

Token-level encryption

OAuth tokens encrypted with AES-GCM using keys held outside the database.

Full audit trail

Every action logged with timestamp, actor, and context. Export-ready for compliance.

No model training

Your data is never used to train AI models — by us, Anthropic, Google, or anyone.
